# NID Request

## Who can apply
Citizens who are of the minimum age for a national ID card can apply for a new NID.
Choose the category "NID Request" when you submit your request.

## Documents usually required
Birth certificate, proof of address, and a passport-size photo. Upload scanned copies in the attachment box.
Requirements can change, so an officer may ask for extra documents in the comments.

## Corrections and replacement
For a lost, damaged or incorrect NID, submit an NID Request and explain the problem in the description and remarks.
Attach a police general diary copy if the card was lost.
