# Passport Request

## New passport
Submit a "Passport Request" with your full name and contact details in the description.
Attach your NID or birth certificate and a recent photo.

## Renewal
For renewal, mention your old passport number in the description and attach a copy of the old passport.
Set the priority to High only for urgent travel and explain the travel date in the remarks.

## Processing
An admin validates the request first, then assigns an officer. You can follow every step on your dashboard.
