# Police Verification

## What it is
Police verification (police clearance) confirms that you have no criminal record. It is often needed for jobs and visas.

## How to apply
Submit a "Police Verification" request and state the purpose (job, visa, etc.) in the description.
Attach a copy of your NID or passport and a recent photo.

## Time needed
Time depends on the workload of officers. Use the priority field honestly; High is meant for urgent deadlines.
