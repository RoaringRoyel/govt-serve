# Account Help

## Registration
Register with your name, email, phone number and a password of at least 8 characters.
Email and phone number must be unique.

## Login problems
Check your email and password. Too many failed attempts in one minute will temporarily block login from your network;
wait a minute and try again.

## Attachments
Allowed files: PDF, PNG, JPG, DOC and DOCX up to 5 MB each.
